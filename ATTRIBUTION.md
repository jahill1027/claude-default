## Proprietary content — personal use only

This personal build also bundles content from the *Tales of the Valiant
Player's Guide* (extra subclasses and options) that is **NOT** openly licensed.
Those records are tagged `source: 'proprietary'` and kept in separate data
files. **This build must not be published or distributed.** A shareable,
open-only build can be produced by setting `VITE_BUILD_PROFILE=shareable`, which
excludes every proprietary record.
